# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Warm Words works without an account or online service.

## Information handled by the app

Warm Words stores the following information locally on the user's iPhone:

- favorites;
- language and category choices;
- reminder status, time, weekdays, and delivery categories;
- personal quotes created by the user; and
- first-run and app preference settings.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to a Warm Words server unless the user explicitly shares a personal quote as described below. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, cloud synchronization, or third-party software development kit.

Local app data remains on the device until the user changes or deletes it, resets the relevant setting, or removes the app. iPhone backups are controlled by the user's Apple and device settings.

## Notifications

Warm Words asks for notification permission only after the user chooses to set or test a reminder. Reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by iOS. The user can change the schedule in Warm Words and can revoke notification access in iOS Settings.

## Sharing

When the user selects Share, Warm Words creates a quote-card image and a link, then presents the standard iOS share sheet. The user chooses whether and where to send them. Links for included quotes contain only a quote identifier and language. Links for personal quotes contain the quote text after `#` so the recipient can preview it and choose whether to save it locally. That fragment is not sent to the Warm Words web host, but it is visible to the selected sharing service and recipient and should not be treated as secret.

Warm Words does not receive the selected destination, recipient details, or shared content. The selected destination handles the items under its own terms and privacy policy. A recipient without the app may see a static download page with no analytics; after installing, the recipient must return to the original message and open the link again.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the [Warm Words support page](https://krazel.github.io/warm-words/support/).

The support page and its dedicated support email address must be live before this policy is used in App Store Connect. Do not replace them with a public repository, personal account, or personal contact details.

## Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that version is distributed.

Release gate: verify this policy against the exact signed archive submitted to Apple.
